At the Table

Everything you need during play. The chapters have the details.

The roll

Roll 5 dice; each 5 or 6 is a hit. At most +2 bonus dice. Damage = hits + bonuses (only with at least one hit). Guard met (hits at least the Guard): clean. Fewer hits: hit back: lose its Harm, gain 1 Chi (unless you went Down). Critical: Guard met and two or more 6s: +1 damage, +1 Chi. A Strike that destroys the enemy can’t be hit back.

A round

1. Wellspring and Harmony share out. 2. Fighters act, any order. 3. Enemies: the Warden or Heart draws 1 per standing Still; packs Swarm (never on a Down fighter); the Warden or Heart Surges. 4. Awakenings count down; check the clocks; the group may withdraw from an Anchor.

On your turn

One action: Strike, or Rally a Down fighter (up with 2 Vigor and back in play; acts next round). Free: rub the Chi off your sheet as you spend it. Chi die (1 Chi, +1 die, one per roll), Help (1 Chi, +1 die to another’s roll; each helper once per roll), your technique (1 Chi, with a Strike). No movement: strike any enemy.

Feeding

Ring: Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water → Wood, skipping missing elements. If the last fighter’s action in this fight was by the fighter before you in the ring: +1 damage (if you hit). A Down fighter keeps their place but takes no actions.

A Strike, in order

Declare technique and bonus dice; roll; reroll (Tide, then Lucky); count hits; damage (and Sweeper); Renewal; hit back (and Counter); Critical Chi.

Losing Vigor

Guardian may take it, if they have more Vigor than the loss (+2 Chi if they lose 2 or more); then Bulwark; then Flow; then Unbreakable. Never above your maximum. At 0: Down: lose all Chi, Awakening ends, and you are out of play until a Rally, a Wellspring share or the end of the fight stands you up (no actions, Help, losses, Chi or powers; nobody can choose you for anything else). After the fight: stand with 1 Vigor.

Shells

A Warden’s Guard is 3 until its Shell shatters, but 2 for the element that shatters it. That element’s first hit shatters it; so does half Toughness (rounded down). Sweeper’s damage isn’t a hit.

Techniques (1 Chi)

Wood: after the roll, you or a standing ally +1 Vigor. Fire: +2 damage if you hit. Earth: a fighter’s next loss this round is 3 less (one at a time). Metal: each 6 is three hits. Water: reroll up to three misses.

Chi

Start 2, max 5. Gain: 1 when hit back (even if a Guardian takes it); 1 on a Critical; 3 for everyone when the Heart fight begins. Spend: Chi die, Help, technique (1); Awaken (3).

Awakening

Once a night, start of your turn, 3 Chi. Open once someone has gone Down tonight, or at the Heart. Lasts 3 rounds counting this one (Seal 2); ends if you go Down or the fight ends.

The night

First Clash: 2 Stills per fighter, no Hour. Hunt, 4 Hours: each Hour, an Anchor (Warden + 1 Still per fighter) or a Respite (full Vigor). Long fight (past round 4, even if you withdraw): +1 Hour, or the Hour is owed (dawn after round 6). Anchor fights and the First Clash end after round 7. Withdraw from an Anchor at the end of any round. Heart (after the four Hours): +3 Chi each, no withdrawing, dawn after round 7.

Endings

Win: the Heart falls. Driven off: dawn with the Heart at half or less. Loss: everyone Down at the Heart, or dawn with the Heart above half.
