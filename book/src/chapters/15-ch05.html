Chapter 5

The Night

Midnight to dawn: six hours. The Unmade only needs one of them to go wrong for you.

Every night of Nightwake has the same shape: a Prologue, the Hunt and the Heart, then an Epilogue. The Storyteller keeps the clocks on the Night sheet, where everyone can see them.

SceneTime
Setup: fighters and difficulty20 minutes with ready-made fighters, 30–40 if anyone builds their own
Prologue and the First Clash25 minutes

The Prologue

Go round the table. Each player describes, in a few sentences, the moment their element woke: where they were, what happened, what they did next. The Storyteller asks one question of each (Chapter 7 has some).

Then the Unmade finds them. The First Clash is a fight against a pack of 2 Stills per fighter, with no Warden. It throws the fighters together and teaches the rules. It costs no Hour, and it is won when the last Still falls. If everyone goes Down, or it reaches the end of round 7, the Stills scatter: Down fighters stand up with 1 Vigour and the Hunt begins anyway. Nobody can withdraw from it.

The Hunt

The Unmade has taken hold of the city at five places, its Anchors. Each Anchor has a Warden, and each Warden wears a Shell of a different element (Chapter 6). The Storyteller tells the players all five Anchors and their Shells at the start of the Hunt.

The Hunt lasts 4 Hours. At the start of each Hour, the group chooses one:

  • Go to an Anchor. Fight its Warden and a pack of 1 Still per fighter. If the Warden falls, the Anchor breaks.
  • Take a Respite. Hide, breathe, patch each other up. Every fighter returns to full Vigour. Chi is unchanged.

The Hunt’s clocks

  • Long fight: an Anchor fight still going at the end of round 4 costs one more Hour, even if the group then withdraws (the clocks are checked first). If no Hours are left, the Hour is owed: dawn comes one round sooner at the Heart.
  • The Warden holds: an Anchor fight still going at the end of round 7 ends, and the Anchor stands.
  • Withdraw: at the end of any round, the group can withdraw. The Anchor stands, and Down fighters stand up with 1 Vigour. Going back later is a new fight: the Warden is whole again, its Shell too, with a fresh pack.
  • Everyone Down: the Anchor stands, and everyone stands up with 1 Vigour.
  • The Hunt ends when the four Hours are spent. With four Hours and five Anchors, at least one Anchor always stands at the Heart.

Losing an Anchor fight still costs the Hour, and the Anchor stays standing to strengthen the Heart. Withdrawing keeps your Vigour; being beaten doesn’t. Neither is ever better than winning.

The Heart

When the Hunt ends, wherever they are, the fighters are drawn to the Heart. They fight it and a pack of 1 Still per fighter.

  • Every Anchor still standing makes the Heart tougher (Chapter 6). Its Toughness is fixed as the fight begins.
  • As the fight begins, every fighter gains 3 Chi (with Breath’s 4, still no more than 5), and anyone who hasn’t Awakened tonight may Awaken at the start of their turn.
  • Nobody can withdraw from the Heart.
  • Dawn breaks at the end of round 7, or round 6 if a long fight left an Hour owed (only a long fight in the last Hour of the Hunt can).

Endings

EndingWhenWhat it means
WinThe Heart fallsThe clocks start again. Whatever the Unmade took comes back with the sun.
Driven offDawn breaks with the Heart at half its Toughness or lessThe Unmade retreats into the dark. The city is scarred, some of what it took stays taken, and it will come back.
LossEvery fighter is Down at the Heart, or dawn breaks with the Heart above halfThe Unmade takes hold. The sun rises on a city that has stopped.

Everyone Down before dawn is a loss, even with the Heart at half or less. “Half” always rounds down: half of 27 is 13.

The Epilogue

Go round once more. Each player says where their fighter is when the sun comes up, and one thing they will never tell anyone about tonight. Chapter 7 has an epilogue for each ending.

What wins nights

  • Walk the ring. Act in cycle order and nearly every Strike is fed.
  • Thin the Stills, but don’t chase the last one (even at the Heart). Every standing Still heals the Warden or Heart, and Stills crumble when their master falls.
  • Rest at about two-thirds. Take a Respite whenever the group ends a fight below about two-thirds of its Vigour; for most groups that means after each hard Anchor fight. Resting much later loses nights; resting much earlier leaves too many Anchors standing. The Heart comes straight after the last Hour, with no rest: if the group starts the last Hour below about two-thirds of its Vigour, spend it on a Respite.
  • Go where you shatter the Shell. Pick the Anchors whose Shells your elements shatter.
  • Spend Chi, save the Awakening. See Chapter 4.
