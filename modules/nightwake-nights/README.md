# Nightwake: Ready-made Nights

The two ready-made nights from the Nightwake rulebook, for Foundry VTT: **The Night the Clocks Stopped** and **The Night the Tide Stood Still**.

**Needs** the Nightwake game system (version 0.2 or later) and Foundry VTT 13 or 14.

## Installing
1. Unzip the download into your Foundry data folder's `Data/modules/` folder, so that you have `Data/modules/nightwake-nights/module.json`.
2. Restart Foundry, open your Nightwake world, and turn the module on under **Manage Modules**.

## Playing
- On the Night sheet, press **Begin the night** and choose a night under **Anchors**. The Night sheet is set up with that night's Anchors and Wardens.
- Press **Read the night** on the Night sheet, or open the **Ready-made Nights** compendium, to read the scenes, the Anchors and the Heart as you play. Players can't open it.
