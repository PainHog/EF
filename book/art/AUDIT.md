# Art audit, version 0.1

Every piece in `book/art/` checked against `ART-CHECKLIST.md`, with the mandatory object and character tables. All pieces are generated by `book/tools/art-gen/scenes/nightwake.mjs` and were rendered with `book/tools/preview-art.mjs` and looked at, then looked at again in place in the built book.

**How figures are built (all scenes):** one figure kit draws every person. Each figure has 1 head, 1 neck, 1 torso, 2 arms ending in 2 fists (closed hands; no fingers are drawn at this scale), and 2 legs ending in 2 feet on the ground line, with a contact shadow. Fists are hands (checklist 9: "a human with no hands" is the defect; a fist is a hand). Fighters are dark silhouettes with an element-coloured glow; Stills are grey, with a dashed light edge on the torso (the "peeled outline" of the text). Nobody recurring is drawn with a face, so no character reference sheets are needed (checklist 10). The five ready-made fighters are shown only by emblems.

**Elements (checklist 18, 21):** each element keeps one colour and one shape everywhere: Wood green with a tall leaf, Fire red with a flame, Earth ochre with a layered square, Metal silver with a ring, Water blue with three waves. The shape alone identifies the element in black and white.

**Text in pictures (checklist 20):** none. Clocks have ticks only; sheets have ruled lines; the map has no labels.

## Not drawn, on purpose
- **No faces, no named characters:** the players picture their own fighters (DESIGN D35), and no element is tied to any look (checklist 23).
- **No echoes:** no black tendrils (Top Cow), no silhouette "shadow men" with glowing eyes (the 2001 film), no nations or elemental-master costumes (checklist 24).
