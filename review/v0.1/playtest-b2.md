### The five fighters

Picked to cover as many options as possible and to put the Guardian, Bulwark, Quick, the Seal, Counter, Flow and Stubborn in the same group. No two fighters share a pick, so no clash roll was needed (the clash roll is drilled in D1).

| Fighter | Element (technique) | Style | Perk | Awakening | Vigor / Chi |

Nobody picked **Brawler**, **Medic** or **Wellspring**, so those are drilled (D2 to D4, D20). The ring is Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water → Wood (Ade → Rosa → Hal → Kit → Nadia). After Kit's Quick Strike the group always acted in the order Nadia, Ade, Rosa, Hal, Kit, so that every Strike after the first was fed.

**How the table rolled:** each fighter's declaration (target, technique, Chi die, Help) was fixed before their dice were rolled. Several fighters were sometimes rolled in one call, but only when none of them had a choice to make after the roll and every target was fixed in advance. Ade's Lucky and Nadia's Tide were always rolled on their own, before anyone later in the round.
