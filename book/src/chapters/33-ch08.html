Chapter 8

The Night the Clocks Stopped

A ready-made night for any city: five Anchors, a Heart and everything you need to run it tonight.

Set it in your own city, or in no city in particular. Everything here exists somewhere in every city big enough to have a night bus.

Midnight

At two minutes to midnight, every clock in the city stops. Phones freeze on 23:58. The trains stop between stations. Most people don’t notice: they stop too, mid-step, mid-word, standing still where they were, as if waiting for something. Only five people keep moving.

Run the Prologue (Chapter 7). Then begin the First Clash.

The First Clash: the night bus station

The fighters are drawn, one by one, to the bus station: the last place in the city with its lights on. A night bus stands at the stop, its passengers frozen in their seats. Their reflections are getting off.

Enemies: a pack of 2 Stills per fighter, no Warden · Won when the last Still falls · Costs no Hour

When the last reflection breaks, the station clock’s hands twitch. From the roof the fighters can see five places where the city has gone grey. The Hunt begins.

The five Anchors

Read the players all five. The order is theirs.

The Old Greenhouse

Shell: Wood · Broken by: Metal

The Victorian glasshouse in the park. Every plant inside has turned the colour of ash and stopped growing mid-curl. The Warden is the Gardener: a hunched giant of grey roots and ironwork, the glasshouse’s frame grown into its back.

On arrival: the glass panes hang loose in the air above the roof, like a held breath.

The Substation

Shell: Fire · Broken by: Water

The power station by the canal, humming without making a sound. The Warden is the Stoker: a figure of cold grey flame, its fire frozen into the shape of fire, shedding sparks that hang where they fall.

On arrival: every street light for a mile is lit but gives no light.

The Underpass

Shell: Earth · Broken by: Wood

The pedestrian tunnel under the ring road, tiled and echoing. The Warden is the Mason: brickwork and paving slabs stacked into the shape of a person, too wide for the tunnel, its footsteps leaving no echo.

On arrival: the graffiti has drained off the walls and lies in a grey puddle on the floor.

The Iron Bridge

Shell: Metal · Broken by: Fire

The old railway bridge over the river, with a freight train stopped halfway across. The Warden is the Girder: a towering frame of riveted iron and coupling chains, the train’s headlamp burning grey in its chest.

On arrival: the rails ring like a struck bell, once, and keep ringing in your teeth.

The Reservoir

Shell: Water · Broken by: Earth

The great old reservoir behind the waterworks. Its surface stands up off the ground in a sheet. The Warden is the Overflow: a wave that never broke, grey and glassy, with the shapes of things it swallowed turning slowly inside it.

On arrival: the rain stopped falling an hour ago; the drops are still up there.

Every Anchor: its Warden and a pack of 1 Still per fighter · Warden numbers in Chapter 6

The Heart: the clock tower

In the last Hour, every stopped clock in the city points the same way: to the old clock tower in the square. The square is full of frozen people, all looking up. Above them the clock face has gone dark, and in the dark something turns very slowly, like a great wheel with nothing on it. That is the Heart.

For each Anchor still standing, show its piece in the Heart: roots through the clock’s works, cold fire behind its face, brick, iron, a hanging sheet of water.

Enemies: the Heart and a pack of 1 Still per fighter · Toughness and Surges from the Night card · Dawn at the end of round 7, sooner for each Hour owed

Morning

Use the epilogues in Chapter 7. If the Heart falls, the clock tower strikes midnight six hours late, and the city starts moving again, mid-step, mid-word, as if nothing happened. Only five people know otherwise.
