Chapter 5

The Night

Midnight to dawn: six hours. The Unmade only needs one of them to go wrong for you.

Every night of Nightwake has the same shape: a Prologue, the Hunt and the Heart, then an Epilogue. The Storyteller keeps the clocks on the Night sheet, where everyone can see them.

SceneAt the table
Setup: fighters and the Night card20–30 minutes
Prologue and the First Clash25 minutes

The Prologue

Go round the table. Each player describes, in a few sentences, the moment their element woke: where they were, what happened, what they did next. The Storyteller asks one question of each (Chapter 7 has some).

Then the Unmade finds them. The First Clash is a fight against a pack of 2 Stills per fighter, with no Warden. It throws the fighters together and teaches the rules. It costs no Hour, and it is won when the last Still falls.

The Hunt

The Unmade has taken hold of the city at five places, its Anchors. Each Anchor has a Warden, and each Warden wears a Shell of a different element (Chapter 6). The Storyteller tells the players all five Anchors and their Shells at the start of the Hunt.

The Hunt lasts 4 Hours. At the start of each Hour, the group chooses one:

  • Strike an Anchor. Fight its Warden and a pack of 1 Still per fighter. If the Warden falls, the Anchor breaks.
  • Take a Respite. Hide, breathe, patch each other up. Every fighter returns to full Vigor and every Down fighter stands. Chi is unchanged.

The Hunt’s clocks

  • Long fight: an Anchor fight still going at the end of round 4 costs one more Hour. If no Hours are left, dawn comes one round sooner at the Heart instead.
  • The Warden holds: an Anchor fight still going at the end of round 7 ends, and the Anchor stands.
  • Withdraw: at the end of any round, the group can withdraw. The Anchor stands, and Down fighters stand up with 1 Vigor.
  • Everyone Down: the Anchor stands, and everyone wakes with 1 Vigor.
  • The Hunt ends when the Hours run out, or when every Anchor is broken.

Losing an Anchor fight still costs the Hour, and the Anchor stays standing to strengthen the Heart. Withdrawing keeps your Vigor; being beaten doesn’t. Neither is ever better than winning.

The Heart

In the last Hour, wherever they are, the fighters are drawn to the Heart. They fight it and a pack of 1 Still per fighter.

  • Every Anchor still standing makes the Heart tougher (Chapter 6). Its Toughness is fixed as the fight begins.
  • As the fight begins, every fighter gains 3 Chi, and anyone may Awaken.
  • Nobody can withdraw from the Heart.
  • Dawn breaks at the end of round 7, one round sooner for each Hour owed by a long fight.

The Epilogue

Go round once more. Each player says where their fighter is when the sun comes up, and one thing they will never tell anyone about tonight. Chapter 7 has an epilogue for each ending.

What wins nights

  • Walk the ring. Act in cycle order and nearly every Strike is fed.
  • Thin the Stills, but don’t chase the last one. Every standing Still heals the Warden or Heart, and Stills crumble when their master falls.
  • Rest at about two-thirds. Take a Respite when the group is down to about two-thirds of its Vigor. Resting much later loses nights; resting much earlier leaves too many Anchors standing.
  • Break the Anchors you can break. Pick the Anchors whose Shells your elements break.
  • Spend Chi, save the Awakening. See Chapter 4.
