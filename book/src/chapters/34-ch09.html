Chapter 9

Making Your Own Night

Every city has a place where the water is held back, a place where the iron is old, and a place where the trees remember what was there before the streets. That is where the Unmade will take hold.

Building a night

  1. Pick or roll the First Clash on its table.
  2. Make five Anchors, one for each Shell. For each element, pick or roll a place on that element’s table. Each entry names its Warden.
  3. Pick or roll a sign of the Unmade for each Anchor: what the fighters see when they arrive. If another Anchor already has it, take the next sign down the table (after 6, go back to 1).
  4. Pick or roll the Heart.

Or make up any of them, as Chapter 10 does: keep one Anchor of each Shell, and give each a Warden and a sign.

Always five Shells

Every night has exactly five Anchors, one for each Shell element, so every fighter’s element has a Shell to shatter and no group is left without one. Every result on these tables can be used in any night. If you roll a place your city doesn’t have, take the nearest thing it does.

The numbers never change: every Warden is a Warden (Chapter 6), whatever it is made of. What you are choosing is the city, the picture and the story. Chapter 10 is a night built from these tables and a few ideas of its own.

The First Clash

d6Where the fighters meet
1The night bus station: the passengers’ reflections are getting off.
2An all-night launderette: the shadows in the drums are climbing out.
3A 24-hour supermarket: the security monitors show grey shoppers who aren’t there.
4An all-night diner: every customer’s outline stands up and leaves its body behind.
5A nightclub queue: the music stops; the queue’s shadows keep dancing towards you.
6The last train home, stopped between stations: something is walking down the carriages.

Signs of the Unmade
