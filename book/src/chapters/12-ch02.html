Chapter 2

Fighters

An hour ago you were somebody ordinary: finishing a shift, catching the last bus, arguing about whose turn it was to take the bins out. Then the clocks stopped, and something in you started.

Making a fighter

Each fighter makes four picks:

  1. an element: Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal or Water;
  2. a fighting style: how you fight;
  3. a perk: something you bring from your ordinary life;
  4. an Awakening: what you become when everything is on the line.

No two fighters in a group share any pick: one fighter per element, and each style, perk and Awakening at most once. Everyone chooses all four picks, then any clash is settled pick by pick: everyone who wants it rolls a die and the highest roll keeps it (roll again on a tie). The others change only that pick, to any one nobody has (or roll for it, as below); a ready-made fighter keeps the rest of theirs.

Rather leave it to the dice? Once the others have chosen and settled any clashes, roll a die for each pick, counting down the lists in this chapter (for elements, reroll a 6). If it’s taken, take the next free one down the list (after the last, go back to the first).

A tip, not a rule: a strong group can shatter two or three different Shells (Chapter 6), and most groups want at least one fighter whose picks help the others: Guardian, Medic, Wellspring, Harmony or Wood’s Renewal. Anyone can Help.

Then give your fighter a name, one line about who they were before tonight, and say what they were doing when their element woke. Write it all on your fighter sheet, with your starting Vigor and 2 Chi.

In a hurry? Take one of the ready-made fighters at the end of this chapter. With them, the fighters are ready in five minutes.

Starting Vigor

Fewer fighters means each of them carries more of the night. Your starting (and maximum) Vigor depends on how many fighters there are when the night begins:

Starting Vigor is 10 with three fighters, 7 with four and 6 with five.

Every fighter starts with 2 Chi and can hold at most 5.

Elements

Your element is what you awaken to. It decides your place in the two cycles (Chapter 3 and Chapter 6) and gives you a technique: spend 1 Chi as you declare a Strike to use it, once per Strike. Describe your element however you like; the rules care only about the technique.

Wood

“Push through the concrete. Bend with the wind. Grow back.”

Growth, patience, the wind. Roots split pavement at your feet; a gust carries you over a fence.

Technique: Renewal

After the roll, you or an ally who isn’t Down regains 1 Vigor, whatever the result.

Feeds: Fire · Shatters: Earth Shells

Fire

“Everything burns brighter right before it ends. So burn.”

Heat, speed, passion. Your fists leave trails of sparks; the air shimmers around you.

Technique: Blaze

+2 damage if you hit at all.

Feeds: Earth · Shatters: Metal Shells

Earth

“I’m not moving. You can go round.”

Weight, steadiness, shelter. The ground rises to meet a blow meant for a friend.

Technique: Bulwark

Choose a fighter, you or an ally. The next time they would lose Vigor this round, they lose 3 less. A fighter carries one Bulwark at a time: you can’t give one to a fighter who already has one.

Feeds: Metal · Shatters: Water Shells

Metal

“One clean cut beats a hundred wild swings.”

Precision, edges, resolve. Light glints along your hands like the edge of a blade.

Technique: Edge

Each 6 you roll counts as three hits.

Feeds: Water · Shatters: Wood Shells

Water

“Missed? Let it flow round again.”

Flow, depth, second chances. You move like a current; a blow that misses simply comes back.

Technique: Tide

After the roll, reroll up to three dice that aren’t hits.

Feeds: Wood · Shatters: Fire Shells

Fighting styles

Your fighting style is how you fight: what you learned, or what your body just knows tonight. Each one is always on.

StyleEffect
Brawler+1 damage when you meet the Guard.
GuardianOnce per round, when another fighter would lose Vigor and you have more Vigor than the loss (as it comes, before any protection), you may lose it instead. If you lose 2 or more Vigor this way, gain 2 Chi.
SweeperWhen you meet the Guard, also deal 1 damage to one other enemy, if there is one.
CounterWhen an enemy hits you back, deal it 1 damage and gain 1 more Chi.
FlowLose 1 less Vigor each time, except from a Sweep.
BreathGain 4 Chi at the start of every fight.

Reading the styles

  • Guardian takes the whole loss as a loss of your own: the other protections (Chapter 3) then apply to you, and a Bulwark on the fighter you saved waits for their next loss. A Sweep’s losses come one at a time, so a Guardian can take one other share as well as their own.
  • Sweeper and Counter damage can finish an enemy. If Counter destroys a Warden or the Heart, the fight is won.
  • Sweeper’s damage isn’t a hit: it never counts as the first hit that shatters a Shell, though it can bring a Warden to half its Toughness. With no other enemy (the First Clash, or once the pack is gone) it does nothing.
  • Counter’s damage lands even when the hit back puts you Down (then you gain no Chi), and Counter answers even when a Guardian takes the loss for you.
  • Flow works against hit backs, Crushes and Swarms, but not Sweeps.
  • Breath can’t take you past 5 Chi.

Perks

Your perk is something you bring from your ordinary life into the strangest night of it.

PerkEffect
Tough+3 maximum Vigor.
MedicAfter each fight, share out 2 Vigor among the fighters.
LuckyOnce per fight, after you roll, reroll every die that isn’t a hit.
QuickBefore the first round of each fight, take a free Strike with +1 die. It can’t be hit back.
StubbornThe first time in each fight you would go Down, stay up instead, with 2 Vigor (or 1, if that was all you had).
TrainedYour Criticals deal 2 more damage and give 1 more Chi.

Reading the perks

  • Quick’s free Strike is a normal Strike (technique, Chi die and Help allowed, two bonus dice at most), but it isn’t a turn and isn’t part of round 1: you can’t Awaken before it, a Bulwark given with it ends before round 1, and its damage doesn’t count for round 1’s Crush (it still feeds the next action). It comes after the Chi a fight begins with (Breath, and the Heart’s 3).
  • Medic shares out after Down fighters have stood up, and you may keep some for yourself.

Awakenings

Once a night, when things are at their worst, you can become more than you are. Chapter 4 explains when and how; here is what each one does while it lasts.

AwakeningWhile it lasts
AscendantEach of your hits deals 2 damage.
UnbreakableYou lose no Vigor.
WellspringAt the start of each round, share out 2 Vigor. A Down fighter given any stands up.
TempestYou take two actions each round, and your Strikes can’t be hit back.
SealThe Warden or the Heart can’t Surge. Holding the Seal is your action each round. Lasts 2 rounds.
HarmonyAt the start of each round, share out 3 Chi.

Ready-made fighters

These five are built to work together in any combination: any three, four or all five of them make a good group. Rename them, change who they are, keep the picks. A group made from them is a little stronger than an average one: three of them win about 85 nights in 100 at Standard and all five about 77, so for a real challenge, play Hard.

Dani Okafor

“Shortcut. Trust me.”

Nineteen, a bike courier who knows every alley in the city. The wind woke first: it lifted her off the saddle mid-delivery and set her down on a rooftop.

Wood · Sweeper · Quick · Ascendant

Marcus Bell

“Everybody behind me. Now.”

Fifty-eight, caretaker of a crumbling tower block, who knows every tenant by name. He was fixing the boiler when the flame climbed out of it and into his hands.

Fire · Guardian · Stubborn · Harmony

Jun Park

“I knead dough at four every morning. I can take a hit.”

Twenty-eight, a baker and amateur boxer. The kitchen floor shook, the flour settled into a perfect circle around his feet, and the ground has been on his side ever since.

Earth · Brawler · Lucky · Tempest

Priya Raman

“Hold still. This will sting.”

Thirty-four, a paramedic who runs free-running routes on her days off. Her scissors rang like a bell and stayed sharp in her mind long after she put them down.

Metal · Flow · Medic · Wellspring

Sam Whitlock

“Use their weight, dear. They always bring plenty.”

Sixty-seven, a retired judo coach who still teaches the Tuesday class. She was swimming late lengths when the water stopped moving for everyone except her.

Water · Counter · Trained · Unbreakable

Joining late, leaving early

Starting Vigor is set by how many fighters there are when the night begins; the enemies are sized by how many there are when each fight begins. A player who must leave can hand their fighter to another player (or the Storyteller), who plays them by the same rules. A player who arrives late joins at the start of the next fight. Then everyone’s maximum Vigor becomes the starting Vigor for the new number of fighters (+3 if Tough), and anyone above it drops to it; the new fighter makes all four picks from what nobody has and starts at that maximum with 2 Chi. A night has five fighters at most.
