Chapter 4

Chi and the Awakening

Chi is the element moving through you: the heat behind the punch, the stillness before the throw. You will never have much of it, and you should never keep it long.

Chi

You start the night with 2 Chi and can hold at most 5. Any Chi you would gain past 5 is lost.

Gaining Chi

  • 1 each time an enemy hits you back (unless it puts you Down);
  • 1 on a Critical;
  • 3 for every fighter when the Heart fight begins;
  • more from Breath, Guardian, Counter, Trained and Harmony.

Spending Chi

SpendChiEffect
Chi die1A bonus die on your own roll
Help1A bonus die on another fighter’s roll
Technique1Your element’s technique, with a Strike
Awaken3Your Awakening (below)

Losing Chi

You lose all your Chi when you go Down, and some when the Unmade Drains it (Chapter 6). Chi left over at dawn is worth nothing.

Designer’s Note: spend it

In thousands of simulated nights, the groups that spent their Chi as they got it did better than the groups that saved it. You will get it back: every hit back refills you, and the Heart gives everyone the 3 Chi they need to Awaken. A fighter sitting on 5 Chi is wasting every point they gain.

The Awakening

Once a night, you can become what your element has been trying to make you.

Awakening

  • Once per night, at the start of your turn, spend 3 Chi to Awaken.
  • You may Awaken from the moment the first fighter goes Down tonight, or once the Heart fight begins, whichever comes first.
  • It lasts 3 rounds, counting the round you Awaken (Seal lasts 2). It ends early if you go Down or the fight ends.

Your Awakening is the one you picked when you made your fighter. Describe it big: this is the moment the city will remember.

Ascendant

Each of your hits deals 2 damage. Double your hits first, then add bonuses such as feeding or Blaze. Hits still count once against the Guard.

Unbreakable

You lose no Vigor, from anything. Enemies still hit you back when you miss their Guard, so you still gain Chi.

Wellspring

At the start of each round while it lasts, share out 2 Vigor among the fighters, as you choose. A Down fighter given any stands up and acts that round. Since you Awaken on your turn, the first share comes at the start of the next round.

Tempest

You take two actions each round, one after the other, and your Strikes can’t be hit back. Your second action is never fed by your first.

Seal

The Warden or the Heart can’t Surge. Holding the Seal is your action each round, starting with the round you Awaken: you can’t Strike or Rally, but you can still Help. Your held Seal counts as your action for feeding. It lasts 2 rounds, and Stills still Swarm.

Harmony

At the start of each round while it lasts, share out 3 Chi among the fighters, as you choose (no one past 5). The first share comes at the start of the next round.

When to Awaken

Most nights, save your Awakening for the Heart: every fighter gets the 3 Chi to pay for it there, and the Heart is where it matters most. Awaken in the Hunt only when a fight is going badly: someone is Down and the Warden is still strong. Awakening at the first chance simply costs you the Heart.
