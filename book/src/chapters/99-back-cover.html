At two minutes to midnight, every clock in the city stopped.

Five ordinary people kept moving. A courier, a caretaker, a baker, a paramedic, a retired coach: each one awake to an element. Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water.

Something from outside the world has taken hold of the city. It has hollowed out five places and gathered itself into a Heart, and if it is still holding on at sunrise, it will never let go.

Nightwake is a one-night roleplaying game for 3–5 players and a Storyteller. Make a fighter in five minutes, walk the cycle of the five elements, break the Anchors, and tear out the Heart before dawn.

Five elements. One night. Break the Heart before dawn.
