# Playtest B3: Nightwake v0.1, Hard, three experienced players

Date: 2026-10-22. The Storyteller played every seat, strictly by the book (book/src/chapters, the built PDF of 31 pages for page numbers). Every die came from the dice script; every roll is in `playtest-b3-dice.log` with its label. Nothing outside the book was read.

## Summary

- **Blocker: none.** The night and every drill could be played to the end from the book alone.
- **Did the table have to invent a ruling on core flow? Once, and only in a drill.** In the enemies' step, the Crush tie rule ends "if none of them acted, roll a die" without saying how one die picks between two fighters (issue 3; it never came up in the night itself). Everything else on core flow (the round, a Strike, feeding, losing Vigor and going Down, the enemies' step, the clocks, the endings) was answered by the text, including every edge case the test hunts for: Down fighters, the Guardian in a Sweep, feeding around a Down fighter, the start-of-round shares, Quick and the Awakening, Bulwark, Swarm and Crush targets and ties, Counter finishing a fight, everyone Down mid-step.
- Outside core flow the table used three small rulings: when a late arrival's lower maximum Vigor applies (issue 1), Tempest's two actions in the round you Awaken (issue 11), and the exact half of an odd Toughness (issue 5, really just arithmetic).
- **Result: Win on Hard** (the Heart, 51 Toughness with three Anchors standing, fell in round 5 of 7, with two of the three fighters Down).
- **Issues: 0 blockers, 1 confusing, 13 minor.** The three that matter most: (1) joining late lowers everyone's maximum Vigor on arrival, even mid-fight, though the newcomer joins only at the next fight; (7) at the Heart, a group with no Chi at all turns every Drain into a Crush 7, so "always keep 1 Chi on someone" beats the book's own "spend it" advice; (3) with (4): the tie rules are vague in one place and missing from both sheets.

## The table

- Difficulty: **Hard** (the Heart's Crush 7, Sweep 3).
- Night: the ready-made night, "The Night the Clocks Stopped" (Chapter 8).
- Three fighters, so (size table, p21): starting Vigor 10, Warden Toughness 27, Heart Toughness 27 with one Anchor standing and +12 per other, First Clash 6 Stills, 3 Stills at an Anchor or the Heart, a Call fills a pack up to 5.

### The fighters (built to break things; no pick shared, so no clash rolls were needed)

| Fighter | Element | Style | Perk | Awakening | Start |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bo** | Earth (Bulwark) | Guardian | Tough | Wellspring | 13 Vigor, 2 Chi |
| **Lane** | Metal (Edge) | Counter | Trained | Harmony | 10 Vigor, 2 Chi |
| **Kit** | Fire (Blaze) | Breath | Quick | Tempest | 10 Vigor, 2 Chi |

What the players were trying to break:
- **Bo** is a sponge: Tough so the Guardian can take nearly any loss ("more Vigor than that"), Earth so he can Bulwark himself and then take other people's losses through it, and Wellspring to stand Down fighters up at the start of a round.
- **Lane** is a Chi engine: every miss is hit back for Chi, Counter adds a point of damage and a Chi, and if Bo takes the loss Bo gains 2 Chi as well (4 Chi from one miss). Metal plus Trained turns any pair of 6s into a huge Critical. Harmony then pours Chi into Kit.
- **Kit** is the opener: Breath gives 4 Chi at the start of every fight, Quick spends it at once on a free Strike that can't be hit back, and Tempest doubles his actions at the Heart.

Ring: Fire, Earth, Metal (Wood and Water skipped). Kit, Bo, Lane in the ring. Because Kit's Quick Strike comes before round 1 and "two actions in a row by the same fighter don't feed", the table acts **Bo, Lane, Kit** every round: Bo is fed by Kit's Quick Strike (or by Kit's last action of the round before), Lane by Bo, Kit by Lane. Every Strike in the night after the very first is fed.

Shells this group shatters: Metal (Kit: the Iron Bridge), Water (Bo: the Reservoir), Wood (Lane: the Old Greenhouse). Nobody shatters the Substation (Fire) or the Underpass (Earth).

## Issues

Severity: blocker (play cannot go on), confusing (a table would stop and argue), minor (wording, a sheet, or a balance question for the simulator). Page numbers are the built PDF's.

**1. Joining late: maximum Vigor changes on arrival, but the fighter joins at the next fight.** Confusing. p10, "Joining late, leaving early": "When a player arrives late, everyone’s maximum Vigor becomes the starting Vigor for the new number of fighters (+3 if Tough), and anyone above it drops to it. ... and joins at the start of the next fight." If a player walks in during a fight, the others lose maximum Vigor at once (from 10 to 7 or 6) while the new fighter can't help and the enemies keep their size. Fix: "When the late fighter joins (at the start of the next fight), everyone’s maximum Vigor becomes ...". Ruling to use tonight: apply the new maximum when the new fighter joins, not when the player arrives.

**2. Guardian: "more Vigor than that".** Minor. p8: "when another fighter would lose Vigor and you have more Vigor than that, you may lose it instead." "That" could be the other fighter's Vigor or the loss; the card (p30) says "if they have more Vigor than the loss". It also matters that the test uses the loss as it arrives, before the Guardian's own Bulwark (Hour 3: Bo at 2 Vigor with a Bulwark could not take a 2-Harm hit back that his Bulwark would have cancelled). Fix: "and you have more Vigor than the loss (before any Bulwark, Flow or Unbreakable), you may lose it instead". Ruling to use tonight: more Vigor than the loss as it arrives.

**3. Ties nobody acted to break: "roll a die" picks between fighters how?** Minor (rare; only in a drill). p20: "Ties, for a Crush or a Drain: the fighter among them who acted last that round; if none of them acted, roll a die." One die, two or more fighters: who wins? It happens when the tied fighters were Rallied this round (a Rallied fighter acts from the next round), so it is reachable. Fix: "if none of them acted, each rolls a die and the highest takes it (roll again on a tie)", matching the pick clashes on p7. Ruling to use tonight: the Storyteller splits the die's faces evenly among the tied fighters and rolls once.

**4. The tie rule is on neither sheet.** Minor. p29, Night sheet: "Crush: most damage this round, standing fighters only (else most Vigor)"; p30 has no tie rule either. A Crush tie came up three times (the Bridge round 1, D2 round 1, D3 round 7) and each time the Storyteller had to turn to p20. Fix: add "Ties: who acted last (none acted: roll)" to the Night sheet's Crush and Drain rows. Ruling: as p20.

**5. Half of an odd Toughness.** Minor. p20: "It also shatters once the Warden is at half its Toughness or less"; p17: "Dawn breaks with the Heart at half its Toughness or less"; p29 asks the Storyteller to write down the Heart's "half". With three fighters every Warden (27) and every Heart (27, 39, 51) is odd, and five fighters' Wardens are 45. Literally it is 13.5, so 13 or less, but a Storyteller writing "half" on the sheet has to pick 13 or 14. Fix: "half its Toughness (round down) or less" or print the halves in the size table. Ruling to use tonight: exact half, so 13 or less for 27 and 25 or less for 51.

**6. "Anyone may Awaken" at the Heart.** Minor. p17: "As the fight begins, every fighter gains 3 Chi ..., and anyone may Awaken at the start of their turn." A player who Awakened in the Hunt (D1, Kit) can read this as permission to do it again; p14 says "Once per night". Fix: "and anyone who hasn't Awakened tonight may Awaken at the start of their turn". Ruling: once per night wins.

**7. At the Heart, holding 1 Chi stops a Crush 7; the Designer's Note says spend it all.** Minor (balance and advice). p20: "A Drain that finds no Chi Crushes instead (the Heart’s, only when no fighter has any Chi)"; p14: "A fighter sitting on 5 Chi is wasting every point they gain." When the whole group is dry, the Heart Crushes on 1, 2 and 5: half of all Surges, at 7 Harm on Hard. Tonight that is exactly what put Lane Down (Heart round 3). Experienced players will always leave 1 Chi on someone (the Seal holder, or whoever won't spend it). Fix: either add that tip to "What wins nights", or have the Heart's empty Drain do something smaller, after a simulator check. Ruling: as written.

**8. Stubborn heals a fighter at 1 Vigor.** Minor (a free gain, once per fight). p9: "The first time in each fight you would go Down, stay up with 2 Vigor instead." At 1 Vigor, being hit takes you to 2 (D6a2). Since the players choose the Swarm's target, a Stubborn fighter at 1 Vigor is a Swarm sink that gains Vigor. Fix: "stay up instead, with 2 Vigor or the Vigor you had, whichever is less" (simulate first). Ruling: as written.

**9. Which "end of round"?** Minor. p11, the round: "3. Enemies act. ... 4. End of round. Awakenings count down, then the clocks are checked"; but the stat blocks say the draw happens "At the end of each round, before anything else acts" (p20), the Swarm "At the end of each round" (p19) and the Surge "At the end of each round, after the Swarm" (p20); and the card (p30) numbers the same steps 0 to 3. A Storyteller can read "end of round" as step 4 and count Awakenings down before the Surge (which matters for Seal and Unbreakable). Fix: in the stat blocks, "In the enemies’ step" instead of "At the end of each round"; number the card's steps 1 to 4. Ruling: draw, Swarm and Surge in step 3, before anything counts down.

**10. Everyone Down in the round dawn breaks.** Minor. p17 Endings: "Driven off: Dawn breaks with the Heart at half its Toughness or less"; "Loss: Every fighter is Down at the Heart". If the round 7 Surge puts the last fighter Down while the Heart is under half, the fight ends then (p13: "A fight ends the moment ... every fighter is Down, even in the middle of a step"), before dawn, so it is a Loss (D7b showed it in round 4; D8b came within one die of it in round 7). The rules reach the answer, but players who have the Heart under half will feel cheated unless it is said. Fix: one sentence under Endings: "Everyone Down before dawn is a Loss, even with the Heart under half." Ruling: Loss.

**11. Tempest in the round you Awaken.** Minor. p15: "You take two actions each round, one after the other". Wellspring and Harmony say their first share comes next round, and Seal says "starting with the round you Awaken"; Tempest says neither. Fix: "...each round, starting with the round you Awaken". Ruling used: two actions in the round you Awaken (it lasts "3 rounds, counting the round you Awaken").

**12. Swarm sinks and free misses.** Minor (balance check). p19, Swarm: "to one fighter who isn’t Down, of the players’ choice"; p8, Flow: "Lose 1 less Vigor each time, except from a Sweep"; p8, Bulwark: "The next time they would lose Vigor this round, they lose 3 less." Players always pick the best sink: a Flow fighter makes every Swarm of 1 free all night; Unbreakable made all three of D5's Swarms free; a Bulwarked fighter eats one. And an Earth fighter who Bulwarks themself before every Strike gets a free miss: the Bulwark eats the hit back, and the hit back refunds the Chi. None of this is a loop, but the simulator should target Swarms the way these players did. Ruling: as written.

**13. "Don't chase the last one" at the Heart.** Minor (advice). p17: "Thin the Stills, but don’t chase the last one. Every standing Still heals the Warden or Heart, and Stills crumble when their master falls." At the Heart a last Still heals 1 a round for up to 7 rounds and Swarms each round; killing it cost the group one cheap Strike against Guard 1 in round 1, and the Heart never healed again. Fix: say "at an Anchor", or check it in the simulator. Ruling: advice only.

**14. Fighter sheet: an orphaned check box.** Minor (layout). p28, "This fight": "Lucky used ☐ Stubborn used ☐ Down ☐ This round (clear each round): Bulwark on you ☐ Guardian used" and then the box wraps onto the next line by itself at the left, under "Lucky used", where it reads as an unlabelled box. Fix: shorten the line or give "This round" its own line.

## What worked

- **The Strike in order (p12) settled every interaction without argument:** Tide then Lucky on the same roll, Renewal before the hit back, Counter after it, Critical Chi last, and "the Strike is judged against the Guard you rolled against" when a Shell broke mid-Strike (three times).
- **Feeding is airtight.** Quick's Strike feeds the first action of round 1, a 0-hit Strike still feeds, a Rally feeds, a held Seal feeds, a Strike taken just before going Down feeds, Tempest's second action is never fed, and a Down fighter breaks the chain for the fighter after them. Every case came up and every one was in the text.
- **Down is exhaustive.** p12's out-of-play list answered every "can I target them?" question (Swarm, Sweep, Renewal, Help, Harmony shares), and Counter's last answer is called out.
- **The Guardian reading box (p9)** covers the hard cases: the Guardian's own Bulwark applies to a loss they take, a Bulwark on the saved fighter waits, a Sweep lets the Guardian take one other share.
- **The clocks** ran from the Night sheet alone: round 4 and round 7 marked, "Hour owed" and "Awakenings open" boxes, and "even if the group then withdraws (the clocks are checked first)" closes the obvious dodge.
- **Fights ending mid-step**, the Counter clause and "the Drain that finds no Chi" all gave one answer.
- **Late joining** took two minutes: new maximums, picks from what's left, a pick clash rolled off, the ring redrawn.
- **The size table matches the formulas** for three and five fighters (27/39/51 and 50/70).

## How it felt

- **Losing was a real risk, mostly in the Hunt.** The group pushed on below two-thirds Vigor twice, nearly wiped at the Greenhouse (Kit and Lane Down, a forced withdrawal) and had to rest in the last Hour. At the Heart two of three fighters went Down (Lane to a Drain that turned into Crush 7, Bo to a Crush 7) and the last blow was a 54% roll by the only fighter left. But Driven off was already secured in round 3, and the Heart (51, three Anchors standing) fell in round 5.
- **Chi was always spent, and always short.** After the first round of any fight, most Strikes were rolled with no Chi at all. Breath's overflow was lost at three fight starts. Harmony's 3 a round mattered because everyone was dry by round 2. Nobody ever sat on Chi; issue 7 is the one reason they should have.
- **No dominant tactic that breaks the game, but strong habits:** act in ring order with the Quick fighter's free Strike in front so every Strike is fed; Bulwark yourself before you swing; let a Tough Guardian take hit backs (one miss made 4 Chi: 2 for the striker with Counter, 2 for the Guardian) and order Sweep shares so the Bulwark eats the Guardian's own share and the Guardian earns Chi on another's; send every Swarm to Flow, Unbreakable or a Bulwark; kill the Heart's pack in round 1.

Only a Tough Guardian, Unbreakable or a Bulwark answers it. The drill still won in round 5 (the Seal blanked two Surges), so this is a note on feel, not a fault: a designer's note might say so.
- **How long:** 14 fight rounds in the night (First Clash 1, Bridge 3, Reservoir 2, Greenhouse 3, Heart 5) plus a Respite. At a real table that is roughly two and a half hours with setup, inside the book's three.

**Win on Hard.** The three fighters broke the Iron Bridge and the Reservoir, withdrew from the Greenhouse, rested, and broke the Heart (51 Toughness, three Anchors standing) in round 5 of 7, with Bo and Lane Down and Kit standing. In the drills, five fighters on Hard also won in round 5; the branches showed both kinds of Loss (everyone Down, and dawn after round 6 with the Heart above half) and a Driven off.
