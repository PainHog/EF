At the Table

Everything you need during play. The chapters have the details.

The roll

Roll 5 dice; each 5 or 6 is a hit. At most +2 bonus dice. Damage = hits + bonuses (only with at least one hit). Guard met (hits at least the Guard): clean. Fewer hits: hit back: lose its Harm, gain 1 Chi. Critical: Guard met and two 6s: +1 damage, +1 Chi. A Strike that destroys the enemy can’t be hit back.

A round

1. Fighters act, any order. 2. Enemies: the Warden or Heart regains 1 per standing Still; packs Swarm; the Warden or Heart Surges. 3. Awakenings count down; check the clocks.

On your turn

One action: Strike, or Rally a Down fighter (up with 2 Vigor, acts next round). Free: Chi die (1 Chi, +1 die), Help (1 Chi, +1 die to another’s roll, once per roll), your technique (1 Chi, with a Strike).

Feeding

Ring: Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water → Wood, skipping missing elements. If the action just before yours in this fight was by the fighter before you in the ring: +1 damage (if you hit).

A Strike, in order

Declare technique and bonus dice; roll; reroll (Tide, then Lucky); count hits; damage (and Sweeper); Renewal; hit back (and Counter); Critical Chi.

Losing Vigor

Guardian may take it; then Bulwark; then Flow; then Unbreakable. At 0: Down: no actions or Help, lose all Chi, Awakening ends. After the fight: stand with 1 Vigor.

Techniques (1 Chi)

Wood: after the roll, you or an ally +1 Vigor. Fire: +2 damage if you hit. Earth: a fighter’s next loss this round is 3 less. Metal: each 6 is three hits. Water: reroll up to three misses.

Chi

Start 2, max 5. Gain: 1 when hit back; 1 on a Critical; 3 for everyone when the Heart fight begins. Spend: Chi die, Help, technique (1); Awaken (3).

Awakening

Once a night, start of your turn, 3 Chi. Open once someone has gone Down tonight, or at the Heart. Lasts 3 rounds counting this one (Seal 2); ends if you go Down or the fight ends.

The night

First Clash: 2 Stills per fighter, no Hour. Hunt, 4 Hours: each Hour, an Anchor (Warden + 1 Still per fighter) or a Respite (full Vigor, Down fighters stand). Long fight (past round 4): +1 Hour, or dawn 1 round sooner. Anchor fights end after round 7. Withdraw at the end of any round. Heart: +3 Chi each, no withdrawing, dawn after round 7.

Endings

Win: the Heart falls. Driven off: dawn with the Heart at half or less. Loss: everyone Down at the Heart, or dawn with the Heart above half.
