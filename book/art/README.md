# Book art

**Finished art:** the pictures here are placeholders, laid out at their final sizes. A finished picture saved as `final/<name>.png`, `.jpg` or `.webp` replaces its placeholder in the book and in the online version, cropped to the slot's shape so no page moves. `ART-BRIEF.md` is the artist's brief: the look, the file rules, every picture's size (counted from the layout by `../tools/art-slots.mjs`) and what each one shows.

The placeholder art is hand-built SVG, generated by the scripts in `book/tools/art-gen/` and
inlined at build time wherever the chapters say `<figure class="art" data-art="NAME">`.
Every piece must pass `ART-CHECKLIST.md` (including the object-table audit), and every
rendered piece is looked at (`node book/tools/preview-art.mjs <outDir>`) before it is
called done. Every SVG has a `viewBox` and **no** fixed width or height; the CSS sizes it.
No `<image>`, `<script>`, filters such as `feTurbulence`, or external links.

## House style

A city at night, flat colour with ink outlines (docs/DESIGN.md D32, D35). The book
shows places, the elements and the Unmade, not faces: people appear only as small
silhouettes from one figure kit, and the ready-made fighters only as emblems. Each
element keeps one colour **and** one shape everywhere (Wood leaf, Fire flame, Earth
layered square, Metal ring, Water waves). The Unmade is grey. The audit of every
piece is in `AUDIT.md`.

## Shapes

| Kind | Aspect | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Cover | 612×792 (portrait page) | `section.cover` |
| Wide | about 3:1 | chapter vignettes (`.chapter-art`, 1.8in tall), part pages (`.part-art`) |
| Spot | about 4:3 | end-of-chapter spots (placed by the build), back-cover panel |
| Portrait / badge | square | `.entry .portrait`, `.entry .badge`, `.statblock .portrait` |
